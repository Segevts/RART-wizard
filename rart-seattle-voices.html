What Alby and Ann said

Notes from Test 2. Not a score, and not Test 1.

Alby

Easier with generated polygons. You do not draw a polygon on each tile. You assign a value to each road section. No workflow break across the two generated-polygon days.

Overpasses: the polygon is one continuous shape, so the upper piece has to be deleted before the road underneath. A split can throw a large polygon that has to be deleted and tagged again.

Ann

Easiest is the generated polygon. Most of the work is tagging: read the road, assign the RART, fix overlaps. Multi-level roads are the hard part on tiles that already had polygons.

On the new tool, a polygon can snap to another and merge into one shape. She wants the straight split kept, because that is the one they use most.

Both methods can fuse polygons into one big shape. On the generated path the trigger is a split or an overpass. On the new tool the trigger is a snap-merge.
