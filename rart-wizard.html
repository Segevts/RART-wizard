What is the separation from oncoming traffic?

How is your lane separated from the opposing traffic direction?

Tip: Physical separators include concrete barriers, guardrails, medians with curbs, or grass dividers. One-way roads also count as "Physical/No oncoming".

How many lanes in your direction?

Count lanes going in the same direction as you

Note: A road without lane markings may be multi-lane if width exceeds 5.25m and no physical separation from neighboring same-direction lane. Use street-view to validate uncertain cases.

Are there painted lane marks between same-direction lanes?

Check for lane markings separating you from the next lane going in your direction

Remember: Solid lane marks between same-direction lanes still count as "painted" - they don't indicate single lane!

Is there road edge (RE) separation?

Check for physical or painted road edges/borders on the sides
